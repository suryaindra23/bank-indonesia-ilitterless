# COBI design direction

Source: user-requested visual identity at https://ilitterlessindonesia.org/, inspected 25 November 2026. Existing Bibu illustrations and resident workflows follow the supplied prototype.

Palette overridden by the user’s KOBI reference on 25 November 2026: KOBI Blue #0B2D6B, Ocean #2D8CFA, Sky #A9D6FF, White #FFFFFF, Leaf #22C55E, Forest #15803D. Aqua #E0F2FE and Sand #F8FAFC support backgrounds; Charcoal #334155 carries body text. Secondary accents follow the supplied sheet. Montserrat 400–800 throughout. Forest green pill buttons follow the reference; inputs use 12 px radius and content surfaces 14 px radius. Status colors communicate review outcomes. No automatic dark palette.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. Bibu carries the character; responsive hierarchy gives each task space. Mobile uses bottom navigation and a single column. Desktop uses top navigation and columns where tasks benefit from simultaneous visibility. The login focuses on the email form; the resident home focuses on recording an action. Staff pages prioritize pending reviews; BI pages prioritize source-backed pilot indicators.

Use existing assets. No invented testimonials, people, pilot statistics, completed activities, or stock. New accounts start empty. Reward stock starts at zero. Loading, errors, disabled state, validation, and empty lists must describe actual application state. Sound playback is user-triggered. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Community screen: earned points are the focal value, with an aggregate ranking list beside the resident's community summary on desktop and below it on mobile. Five labeled navigation destinations remain visible. Blue identifies the resident's community; text also names it. No individual member names or fabricated activity feed. A group icon represents the explicitly requested community destination.

Activity articles use the four user-requested fields: photograph, writing, involved parties, occurrence date. Submission explains the real email destination and distinguishes delivery from staff curation. Daily points show the actual 1–10 rule per action; the 50-point article award is conditional on approval. Learning management uses plain labeled fields and type-specific upload/link controls; no fabricated video thumbnails, Office previews, or automatic claims that media was watched.

Dedicated staff dashboards follow the supplied BI screenshot's structure: white navigation with an active role, report headline, filters, six indicators, then two reporting columns and a locality table. BI charts use ocean blue, navy, and grey with pink reserved for rejection. Staff dashboard retains operational tabs, including learning management and article curation. Each dashboard has a separate login entry and enforces granted account roles; navigation links never impersonate another role.

The supplied Blue Mascot Sticker Sheet is preserved unchanged in public/brand/bibu-stickers.png. CSS selects each pose for its context. Greetings and content entrances have finite duration; there is no perpetual motion. Reduced-motion disables them. Public signup grants resident access only; separately granted roles share login credentials and each page selects only an authorized role.

Role navigation is a compact group of links in the header, distinct from operational tabs. No full-width aqua container around role links. Tablet uses a left-aligned second row; mobile uses three readable targets with a divider. Active role retains navy fill and aria-current.
